# Example: From Full Survey to Guided Flow

## Situation

A feedback survey shows 18 fields at once.

Users feel pressure before starting.

## Alternative models

- step-by-step flow
- conversational collection
- card-based quick choices plus optional detail
- hybrid: quick rating first, details later

## Validation

Track:

- start rate
- completion rate
- step drop-off
- time to complete
- qualitative feedback
